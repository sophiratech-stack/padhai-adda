Privacy Policy

What we collect, why, where it is stored, who it is shared with, and your rights under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. Last updated: 14 November 2026.

1. Who we are

Padhai Adda decides why and how tutors’ personal data is used on this website. For any question or request about your data, email sophiratech@gmail.com.

2. What tutors share

  • Name and 10-digit phone number.
  • Qualifications, state, city and area (zone, sector or village).
  • Classes, subjects, teaching mode, fees, experience and a short description.
  • Status (Active or Inactive).
  • Teaching location coordinates, captured from your device.
  • A record of your consent: a “Yes” and the date and time you gave it.

3. Why we use it

We use these details only to run your tutor listing: our team reviews it, and once approved and Active it is shown in search so students and parents can call and navigate to you. We keep the consent record to show that you agreed.

4. What is public

Active, approved listings show the details in section 2, except the consent record, to anyone who visits the website. This includes your phone number and teaching location, so students can call and navigate. Anyone can copy public information, so only list details you are comfortable making public.

5. What students and visitors share

Students are not asked for personal data, and the website does not ask visitors to create an account. The website itself does not set cookies or load analytics or advertising scripts. Our hosting and font providers (section 7) may receive technical details such as your IP address when a page loads.

6. Location

Your device location is requested only when you tap the capture button on the listing form. It is stored with your listing as coordinates and shown to students as the place where you teach.

7. Where data is stored and who it is shared with

  • Google Sheets and Google Apps Script: store and process tutor listings and consent records. Google may keep data on servers outside India.
  • Google Maps: opens only when someone taps Navigate. The teaching location is passed to Google Maps in that link, and Google’s own privacy policy applies.
  • Google Fonts: loads fonts when any page opens, so Google receives the visitor’s IP address and browser details.
  • Cloudflare: hosts the website and may log visitor IP addresses for operating and securing it.
  • The public: anyone who visits the website sees active listings, as described in section 4.

Apart from these providers and the public display above, we do not share tutor data with anyone else.

8. Consent and how to withdraw it

We ask for your consent with a tick box on the listing form, and you cannot submit a listing without it. You can withdraw consent at any time by emailing sophiratech@gmail.com with your Teacher ID. We will hide or delete your listing as you ask. Withdrawing consent does not affect what happened before you withdrew it.

9. How long we keep data

We keep your data while your listing exists. If you ask us to delete it, we delete it within 3 days of receiving the request. Submissions that our team rejects are removed from the review list.

10. Your rights

You can ask us for a summary of the data we hold about you and who we share it with, ask us to correct or complete it, and ask us to erase it. You can also nominate another person to exercise these rights if you die or cannot act for yourself. To use any of these rights, email sophiratech@gmail.com with your Teacher ID.

11. Complaints

If you have a grievance about how we handle your data, email sophiratech@gmail.com and we will respond within the time the law allows. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

12. Children

Tutors must be 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect personal data of anyone under 18. If you think we have, email sophiratech@gmail.com and we will remove it.

13. Security and data breaches

We limit access to listing data to administrators, but no online service is completely secure. If a personal data breach affects you, we will tell you and the Data Protection Board as the law requires.

14. Changes to this policy

If we change how we use or share data, we will update this page and its date. Where the law requires, we will ask for your consent again.

See also our Terms of Service, or contact us with questions.
