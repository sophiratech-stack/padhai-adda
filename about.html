About Padhai Adda

A free, simple way to connect local learners and local tutors.

Our mission

Good teaching is often a few streets away. Padhai Adda makes it easy to find it, and gives independent tutors a free place to be seen.

What we believe

Local first

Learning works best when it is convenient and close.

Free access

No fees for families, none for tutors.

Direct and honest

No middlemen, clear information, simple tools.

How we work

Tutors submit a profile, our team reviews it, and approved active profiles appear in search. Locations, subjects and classes are maintained by our team and expand as new areas join.

Who Padhai Adda is for

Students and parents use Padhai Adda to find a tutor near them and call directly. Tutors use it to create a free listing and be seen by families nearby. Learn more on How it Works, in the FAQs or on our blog.
