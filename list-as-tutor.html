Your data and consent

  • What we collect: your name, phone number, qualifications, state, city, area, classes, subjects, fees, teaching mode, experience, description, status and teaching location (coordinates).
  • Why: to show your tutor profile so students and parents can call you and navigate to you.
  • Who sees it: once approved and Active, all of these details, including your phone number and location, are visible to anyone who visits this website.
  • Where it is stored: in a Google Sheet through Google Apps Script, managed by the Padhai Adda team. Google acts as our service provider.
  • Withdraw any time: email sophiratech@gmail.com with your Teacher ID to hide, correct or delete your listing. We delete your data within 3 days of a deletion request.

Before you submit

Use your real name and a working phone number. Capture the location while you are at the place where you teach, so students can navigate to you. Inactive profiles are saved but hidden from search.

Why list as a tutor on Padhai Adda

Padhai Adda is free for tutors: no listing fee and no commission. Families nearby search by area, class, subject and teaching mode, then call you directly from your tutor card.

Your card shows your fees, experience, qualifications, subjects and teaching style, so choose your words with care. Our guide to building a strong tutor profile has practical tips, and How it Works explains what our team reviews.

Tutor listing questions

Is it free to list as a tutor?

Yes. Listing is free and we charge no commission.

When will my profile appear in search?

After our team reviews it, if its status is Active. Inactive profiles are saved but hidden.

Who can list as a tutor?

You must be 18 or older. See our Terms of Service for details.

How do I change my details later?

Email sophiratech@gmail.com with your Teacher ID and the changes you need.

How do I withdraw consent or delete my data?

Email sophiratech@gmail.com with your Teacher ID. We delete your data within 3 days of a deletion request. Read the Privacy Policy for your other rights.
