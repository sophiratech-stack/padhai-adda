How to choose the right local tutor

Start with the subject and class, then check experience and teaching style. Shortlist three tutors, ask for a demo class and confirm the schedule, location and fees before committing. Choose someone close enough that travel does not eat into study time.

On Padhai Adda, pick your state, city and area, then your class, subject and teaching mode. Compare the tutor cards side by side and call the ones that seem right. You can start on the Find Tutor page.

Home, tutor’s place or online?

Home tuition suits younger children who need supervision. A tutor’s place offers a focused setting and often lower fees. Online works well for older students, niche subjects and busy schedules. Many tutors offer more than one mode.

Padhai Adda lets you filter by teaching mode: At Student Home, At Tutor Place or Online. Select one or several to see tutors who match, and ask about a trial class in the mode you prefer.

Building a strong tutor profile

Be specific about the classes and subjects you teach. Mention methods such as weekly tests or doubt sessions, and your availability. A short, honest description earns more calls than a long promotional one.

  • Name the exact classes and subjects instead of saying “all subjects”.
  • Describe how you teach, for example tests, revision or doubt sessions.
  • Keep fees and experience accurate so families can compare fairly.
  • Capture your location while you are at the place where you teach, so students can navigate to you.

Making the most of a demo class

Bring a topic your child finds hard. Watch how the tutor explains, whether they ask questions and how they handle mistakes. Agree on feedback, homework and a monthly review before you start.

  • Does the tutor check understanding before moving on?
  • Is the pace right for your child?
  • Are the schedule, fees and location clear?
  • Will you get regular feedback?

How to compare tutor fees without just picking the cheapest

Tutor fees on Padhai Adda are shown in ₹ per month, but the number alone does not tell you what you get. Before you compare, ask each tutor the same few questions so the fees are like for like.

  • How many sessions a week, and how long is each one?
  • Are tests, notes or doubt sessions included?
  • What happens if a session is missed or rescheduled?
  • Does the fee change before exams?

Weigh the answers against experience and teaching style, not just price. Agree on the details before you start, and remember that Padhai Adda does not handle payments: fees are settled directly with the tutor. Find tutors and compare cards on the Find Tutor page.

Board exam preparation with a tutor: what to ask for

Whether your child studies under CBSE, ICSE or a state board, tutoring works best when exam preparation starts months ahead rather than weeks. When you speak to a tutor, ask how they would plan the year.

  • A syllabus plan that fits the time left before the exams.
  • Regular short tests so gaps show up early.
  • Practice with previous years’ papers and sample papers.
  • Doubt sessions and a revision schedule for the final weeks.
  • A monthly review of progress with you.

Safety tips for parents booking a home tutor

Padhai Adda reviews each listing, but we do not guarantee any tutor, so a few simple habits help keep everyone comfortable.

  • Speak to the tutor by phone and meet them first, ideally with a parent or guardian present.
  • For younger children, arrange home sessions when an adult is at home.
  • If classes are at the tutor’s place, visit it once beforehand.
  • For online classes, keep the device in a shared area of the house.
  • Tell a family member where and when the sessions take place.

Online tuition: how to get the most from it

Online tuition removes travel and opens up tutors beyond your neighbourhood. A few basics make sessions smoother.

  • Use a quiet spot with a steady internet connection.
  • Keep notebooks, a pen and the day’s topic ready.
  • Ask the tutor which app they use and test it before the first class.
  • Keep sessions at a length your child can focus for, and take short breaks.
  • Review notes after each class and ask about anything unclear.
