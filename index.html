Quick answers

How do I find tuition near me?

Open Find Tutor and select your state, city and area along with class, subject and teaching mode. Then compare the tutor cards and call the one that suits you.

Can I find an online tutor in India?

Yes. Select Online as the teaching mode to see tutors who teach online.

Is there any charge to contact a tutor?

Padhai Adda charges nothing and takes no commission. Tutors set their own fees, which you agree with them directly.
