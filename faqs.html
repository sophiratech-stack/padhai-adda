Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers for students, parents and tutors.

Is Padhai Adda free?

Yes. Searching and listing are free, and we charge no commission.

Do I need an account?

No. Students search without signing up. Tutors fill one form.

How do I find a home tutor near me?

Open Find Tutor, choose your state, city and area, then pick the class, subject and teaching mode (At Student Home, At Tutor Place or Online). The tutor cards show fees, experience and qualifications, with Call and Navigate buttons.

Can I find an online tutor?

Yes. Choose Online under Teaching Mode. You can select Online along with the other modes to see more tutors.

How much does a tutor charge?

Each tutor sets their own fee, shown in ₹ per month on the card. Padhai Adda does not set or collect fees, so confirm the schedule and fees with the tutor before you begin. See our guide to comparing tutor fees.

How are tutors verified?

Our team reviews each submission for completeness and quality. We recommend you also meet the tutor and ask for a demo class.

Is it safe to hire a tutor from Padhai Adda?

Our team reviews each listing, but we do not guarantee any tutor. Meet the tutor first, ask for a demo class and, for younger children, arrange sessions where a parent or guardian is present. Read our safety tips for home tuition.

Does Padhai Adda provide tutors or take bookings?

No. Padhai Adda is a directory. You contact the tutor directly by phone and agree on schedule, fees and location with them.

Can I select more than one class or subject?

Yes. Every filter allows one, several or all options.

Which classes and subjects can I search for?

You can filter by class and subject using the options in the search form. The lists are maintained by our team and grow as new areas join. If something is missing, contact us.

Is Padhai Adda available across India?

Padhai Adda is built for students and tutors across India. Tutors appear only where listings have been approved, so if you cannot find your city or area, tell us and we will add it.

Who can list as a tutor?

Anyone aged 18 or older can create a listing, as set out in our Terms of Service. Start on the List as a Tutor page.

Why is my listing not visible?

It may be awaiting review, or its status is Inactive. Inactive profiles are saved but hidden.

How do I change my details?

Email sophiratech@gmail.com with your Teacher ID and the changes you need.

Why does Navigate need a location?

Tutors capture their teaching location once. Navigate opens it in Google Maps.

My city or area is missing.

Tell us through the contact page and we will add it.

Who handles payments?

Fees are agreed directly between tutor and student. Padhai Adda does not handle payments.
