How it Works

Padhai Adda is a simple directory. Here is what happens on each side.

For students and parents

  1. 1

    Open Find Tutor

    No account is needed.

  2. 2

    Set your filters

    Choose state, city, area, class, subject and mode. Multiple choices are allowed.

  3. 3

    Review the cards

    Compare fees, experience, qualifications and descriptions.

  4. 4

    Call or navigate

    Talk to the tutor and arrange a demo class.

For tutors

  1. 1

    Fill the listing form

    Add your details, subjects, classes, fees and teaching mode.

  2. 2

    Capture your location

    One tap saves the place where you teach.

  3. 3

    Get your Teacher ID

    Keep it safe. It identifies your listing.

  4. 4

    Get approved

    Once reviewed, active profiles appear in search results.

What we review

Complete details

Valid 10-digit phone, realistic fees and experience.

Clear description

No misleading claims or promotional spam.

Correct location

A valid teaching location and area.
